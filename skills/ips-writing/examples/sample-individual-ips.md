# Sample Investment Policy Statement: Individual HNW Client

**Client**: Dr. Somchai & Mrs. Narisa Wattanapanich  
**Prepared by**: Antigravity Private Wealth Management  
**Effective Date**: January 15, 2026  
**Account Base Currency**: THB (with USD offshore sub-account)  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
This Investment Policy Statement (IPS) outlines the strategic investment guidelines and asset allocation mandates for the investment portfolio of Dr. Somchai and Mrs. Narisa Wattanapanich. The portfolio is structured to achieve capital appreciation and generate supplemental retirement income while maintaining prudent risk controls.

---

## 2. Client Profile & Financial Background
- **Client Ages**: 52 (Somchai), 49 (Narisa)
- **Occupation**: Senior Medical Specialist & Corporate Executive
- **Total Investable Assets**: THB 50,000,000
- **Retirement Horizon**: Targeted at age 60 (8 years accumulation phase, followed by 25+ years retirement phase).

---

## 3. Investment Objectives

### 3.1 Return Objective
- **Target Real Return**: Net annual real return of **4.5% above inflation** (Headline CPI), translating to an estimated nominal target return of approximately **6.5% - 7.5% per annum** net of all management fees.
- **Distribution Needs**: No distributions required during the next 8 years. Post-retirement, expected annual distribution will be approximately 3.5% of portfolio value.

### 3.2 Risk Tolerance
- **Ability to Bear Risk**: **High** (Substantial discretionary wealth, high ongoing professional income, no mortgage debt, 8-year horizon before retirement).
- **Willingness to Bear Risk**: **Moderate-to-High** (Comfortable with short-term market fluctuations; past experience through major market cycles).
- **Maximum Acceptable Drawdown**: 18% peak-to-trough over any rolling 12-month period.
- **Portfolio Volatility Target**: Annualized standard deviation $\le 11\%$.

---

## 4. Investment Constraints (TTLLU)

- **Time Horizon**: Multi-stage (Stage 1: 8 years accumulation; Stage 2: 25+ years decumulation).
- **Taxes**: Maximize annual tax allowances in Thai tax-saving funds (SSF / RMF); offshore global investments structured to optimize foreign withholding taxes.
- **Liquidity**: Keep THB 2,500,000 (approx. 5%) in liquid cash equivalents and short-term money market funds as emergency reserves.
- **Legal & Regulatory**: All offshore remittances and portfolio structures must comply with Bank of Thailand (BOT) and SEC Thailand foreign investment guidelines.
- **Unique Preferences**: Exclude direct investments in tobacco, defense/weapons, and single-use plastics manufacturers (ESG negative screening preference).

## 6. Rebalancing & Review Policy
- **Rebalancing Trigger**: Semi-annual review or whenever any asset class drifts by $\pm 4\%$ from its target weight.
- **Review Cadence**: Formal performance review meeting conducted bi-annually, with a comprehensive IPS review annually or upon any material life event.
