# IPS Writing Skill (Investment Policy Statement)

This skill guides the agent in drafting, structuring, and reviewing comprehensive, industry-standard **Investment Policy Statements (IPS)** for both individual (HNW/retail) and institutional investors (endowments, foundations, pension funds, corporate treasuries).

---

## Core Capabilities

1. **Client Profiling & Needs Discovery**: Systematic gathering of financial situation, goals, risk appetite, and constraints.
2. **Objective Setting**: Defining clear, quantifiable return objectives (nominal vs. real) and risk tolerances (willingness vs. ability).
3. **Constraint Analysis (TTLLU Framework)**:
   - **T**ime Horizon
   - **T**ax Considerations
   - **L**iquidity Requirements
   - **L**egal & Regulatory Factors
   - **U**nique Needs & Circumstances (ESG, ethical restrictions, concentrated holdings)
4. **Strategic Asset Allocation (SAA) & Rebalancing Policy**: Setting target weights, acceptable tolerance corridors, benchmark indices, and permissible/prohibited instruments.
5. **Governance & Review Cadence**: Documenting roles, duties, reporting cycles, and triggers for policy amendments.

---

## Workflow Steps

### Step 1: Clarify Context & Profile
Before drafting, identify the target investor type and gather core parameters:
- **Type**: Individual / Family Office / Foundation / Endowment / Corporate.
- **Base Currency & Time Horizon**: Investment horizon (short, medium, long-term).
- **Capital & Inflows/Outflows**: Initial portfolio size, planned contributions, or regular distributions.
- **Language**: English or Thai (support both as requested by the user).

### Step 2: Formulate Objectives & Constraints
- Map risk capacity (financial ability) and risk willingness (psychological tolerance).
- Quantify return targets (e.g., Inflation + X%, preservation of capital, aggressive growth).
- Complete the 5 TTLLU constraints systematically.

### Step 3: Establish Asset Allocation Guidelines
- Define target weights (%) and allowable minimum/maximum bands for:
  - Equities (Domestic & Global)
  - Fixed Income (Government, Investment Grade, High Yield)
  - Alternatives (Real Estate, Private Equity, Commodities)
  - Cash & Cash Equivalents
- Specify allowable investment vehicles (ETFs, mutual funds, direct securities, derivatives for hedging only).

### Step 4: Define Monitoring, Benchmarking & Rebalancing
- Establish corridor-based or calendar-based rebalancing (e.g., rebalance when an asset class drifts by $\pm 5\%$).
- Define appropriate benchmark composite (e.g., 60% MSCI World + 40% Bloomberg Global Aggregate).
- State performance review frequency (quarterly performance reports, annual IPS review).

### Step 5: Draft or Review Document
Use the modular templates and guidelines:
- **Template**: Consult [ips-template.md](./resources/ips-template.md) for full section breakdown.
- **Framework & Guidelines**: Consult [framework-guide.md](./references/framework-guide.md) for risk metrics and TTLLU details.
- **Sample Example**: Inspect [sample-individual-ips.md](./examples/sample-individual-ips.md) for formatting and tone.

## Quality Checklist for Reviewing an IPS

- [ ] Are return objectives stated clearly (real vs. nominal)?
- [ ] Is risk tolerance explicitly separated into "ability" and "willingness"?
- [ ] Are liquidity and cash reserve requirements quantified?
- [ ] Do asset allocation target weights sum to 100%?
- [ ] Are allowable rebalancing bands specified for each asset class?
- [ ] Are prohibited instruments and leverage rules explicitly declared?
- [ ] Is there an annual review process and amendment trigger clause?
